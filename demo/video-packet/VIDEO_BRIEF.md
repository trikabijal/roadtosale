# AuditPro Mobile Demo Video Brief

## Objective
Create a 2 to 2.5 minute product demo video from the HTML prototype in `design/RoadToSale_Mobile.html`.

The video should show that AuditPro:
- guides a salesperson through the Road to the Sale
- listens for real sales-process cues
- turns completed items green and missed items red
- coaches the rep in the moment
- feeds the same signals into the manager BI dashboard

## Audience
- dealership principals
- general managers
- sales managers
- automotive technology buyers

## Format
- screen-recorded click-through of the HTML prototype
- clean American English voiceover
- neutral, credible enterprise-demo tone
- no hype voice
- moderate pace

## Product Story
This is not a generic checklist app. It is a dealership-aware mobile coaching and audit tool.

The demo should emphasize:
1. CRM-fed appointment queue
2. dealership-style discovery
3. Honda digital feature library loading
4. separate parked-feature and in-drive-feature coaching
5. trade-in image analysis
6. rep-facing scorecard
7. bridge into the manager dashboard

## Demo Scenario
- Dealership: Riverside Honda
- Sales rep: Marcus Webb
- Customer: Sarah Mitchell
- Primary recommendation: Honda CR-V Hybrid AWD
- Trade-in: 2019 Toyota RAV4

## Visual Priorities
- keep transcript text large and readable
- clearly animate green and red status changes
- feature misses must be obvious
- treat “Honda digital features loaded” as an important moment
- trade photo capture should feel active, not static

## Success Criteria
The viewer should leave understanding:
- what the rep sees
- what the system detects automatically
- how coaching happens during the sale
- how data rolls up into a dashboard after the deal

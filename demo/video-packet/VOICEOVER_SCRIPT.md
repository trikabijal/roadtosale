# Voiceover Script

## Style
- American English
- calm product-demo narration
- clear, confident, not theatrical
- duration target: 2 minutes 20 seconds to 2 minutes 35 seconds

## Script

This is AuditPro, a mobile coaching and audit tool built around the Road to the Sale.

Instead of starting with a blank form, the salesperson begins with a live queue of CRM-fed appointments for the day. Here we can see four scheduled customers, plus a quick option for a new walk-in. For this demo, we’ll open Sarah Mitchell’s appointment.

On the greet, AuditPro is already listening for key sales-process cues. When the rep says, “Before we head out, can I grab you a coffee or water,” the app detects the hospitality offer automatically and marks that step complete. The rep doesn’t have to stop the conversation to fill out a form.

Next is discovery. AuditPro brings up a dealership-style discovery sheet and listens for the customer’s real needs. As Sarah talks, the app captures signals like school runs, highway commuting, better fuel economy, and a preference for all-wheel drive. The point here is not just transcription. The point is confirming that the rep asked the right questions and documented the right needs.

Now the app moves from discovery into vehicle logic. AuditPro loads the dealership’s Honda feature library and matches the customer’s needs to likely vehicles in inventory. In this case, the CR-V Hybrid AWD comes up as the best fit. When the rep says, “Because you mentioned school runs, commuting, and better fuel economy, this is why I picked the CR-V Hybrid,” the app marks vehicle logic complete.

During the walkaround, AuditPro becomes an in-the-moment coaching tool. It shows the features that should be demonstrated while the vehicle is parked. As the rep presents Apple CarPlay, the power tailgate, and cargo flexibility, those items turn green. A missed feature, like heated seats, stays red, so the app is not just tracking what was done, it is coaching what was skipped.

Then during the test drive, the app switches to the features that should be explained while active. When the rep says, “Notice how Lane Assist is working right now,” that item turns green. Blind spot warning turns green as well. Adaptive cruise remains red because it was not demonstrated.

Back at the dealership, AuditPro supports the trade appraisal too. The rep captures the required vehicle photos, and the system identifies visible condition issues like a rear-quarter scratch and front tire wear, then adds those notes to the appraisal record.

At the end, AuditPro gives the salesperson a clear scorecard showing what they did well, what they missed, and where to improve next time. Those same signals also flow into the manager dashboard, where leaders can track patterns, coach reps, and create interventions across the store.

For experienced reps, it’s a self-coaching tool. For newer reps, it shortens ramp-up and makes the Road to the Sale easier to learn consistently.
